# Visual direction

A playful card table, with the deck as the main object. One large photo at a time; controls stay close to the card. No marketing sections.

- Lavender paper `#f1eafa`, purple ink `#3d225e`, deep purple cards `#593485`, pink action `#e3428c`, lime note `#deecac`, muted text `#79628c`.
- Bricolage Grotesque carries the wordmark and heading; DM Sans handles instructions and controls. Local system fonts provide fallbacks.
- A 310px portrait card at desktop scale, gently fanned backing cards, bespoke eye and star artwork. Small side notes disappear on mobile.
- Meme images use contain sizing so captions remain visible. Draw is the dominant pink button; shuffle is outlined; clear is quiet.
- Draw fades the previous card out then reveals the loaded image. Shuffle briefly tilts the card. Reduced-motion uses instant transitions.
- All controls work with keyboard, show focus, and have visible text. Loading and progress use semantic ARIA state.
