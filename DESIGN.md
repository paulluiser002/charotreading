# Charot Reading

Visual direction: an astral reading table with midnight-blue sky, amethyst surfaces, soft stars and warm starlight accents. The engraved card artwork remains authoritative; expressive photographs retain their original colors.

- Midnight `#0c1024`, text `#f2edf9`, starlight `#e2c691`, amethyst `#6d55ad`, muted `#b9b3cf`, translucent violet hairlines.
- Cormorant Garamond for identity and large editorial typography. DM Sans for functional UI and quiet instructions.
- Desktop has a calm left introduction and a right card table. Mobile centers the reading, removing secondary explanatory copy.
- The card back is original SVG engraving: a human-faced sun, crescent moon, eye, radiating strokes and botanical corners. A faint celestial dial grounds the table.
- Faces and photographic expressions supply the symbolism. Cards have no assigned titles, joke captions or generated interpretations. Contain-fit imagery preserves the source photograph.
- Draw uses an amethyst button. Shuffle and Clear are supporting actions. Card reveals use physical dealing and a 3D turn; reduced motion skips animation.
- Every interface string, metadata description and image alt description is English. The collection dialog preserves Pinterest provenance.

## Motion and interaction

Use physical card motion in response to an action. The main card has separate backfaces in a preserved 3D space; each draw slides engraved backs from a dealer’s deck onto the table one at a time. Three- and five-card readings form a row before gathering into a visible fanned hand; a single card moves into place, lifts and turns. Opening the hand staggers its photographic cards; Next reading gathers the hand before the table returns to rest. Shuffle separates ten visible cards into two packets, alternates cards onto a shared stack, then squares the deck in about 1.8 seconds. Temporary visual cards are always removed after the action.

The palette and original artwork remain authoritative. Controls are grouped into a compact reading panel, with the active hand becoming the primary action after a draw. Mobile uses a shorter introduction and smaller resting deck to keep the table and action within reach. Hover tilt is limited to fine pointers. Reduced motion and background tabs finish or bypass visual effects; they never delay saving the reading.

## Brand, arrival and support

The site identity is Charot Reading. A 2.6-second startup scene fans three original engraved backs in 3D, gathers them and dissolves into the table. A small independent timer releases the page even if deck loading fails. Reduced motion shows the page immediately. Two gold orbital line drawings drift slowly behind the paper, with stronger contrast and slightly heavier strokes so their motion stays visible; decorative loops pause in background tabs and respect reduced motion.

The coffee support feature reuses One ODC’s original QR image, gold circular trigger, 3-second pulse, steam delays and cup wiggle. Its accessible dialog provides Buy me a coffee and Hire me, email copy feedback for charotreading.biz@gmail.com and a manual-copy fallback. Support resets to the coffee view each time it opens.

Three- and five-card hands open face down. Each card is a keyboard-accessible button with its own perspective and two backface-hidden surfaces. A 750ms rotation reveals its photograph; source links appear only after reveal. Reveals persist with the held reading across reopening, refreshes and collection expansion. Next reading clears the reveal state.

The centered Open your hand badge uses a solid violet interior, a bright traveling pastel border and a restrained glow. Fullscreen has a dedicated toolbar above the table and stays fixed within reach in expanded view.

The sun emblem rotates continuously over 24 seconds. The wordmark text remains steady. No manual motion toggle is shown.
